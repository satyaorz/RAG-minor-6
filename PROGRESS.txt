SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAMH-RAG is an advanced, hallucination-aware multi-hop RAG system. It utilizes
the novel ART-R (Adaptive Reasoning-Tree Restructuring) architecture to 
dynamically mutate its reasoning path when failures are detected. It features
hybrid retrieval (Vector + Knowledge Graph) with a Source Consensus Coefficient
(SCC) metric to audit and resolve contradictions between source types.
